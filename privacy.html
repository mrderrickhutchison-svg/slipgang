Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SlipGang, operated by Dominion Growth Solutions LLC ("SlipGang," "we," "us"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the SlipGang mobile application and related services (the "App").

What SlipGang is: SlipGang is a bet-tracking and social tool for sports bettors. We are not a sportsbook, we do not accept wagers, and we do not process bets or payments for gambling. Bets are placed by you directly with licensed third-party sportsbooks.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the App:

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Content you add: the bets, picks, and gang activity you enter or share within the App, including odds, stake amounts you choose to record, and your tail/fade actions.
  • Gang information: the display name you choose and the gangs you create or join.
  • Technical data: basic app and device information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core features: tracking your bets, showing your gang's picks, and enabling tail/fade.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To send service-related notifications (for example, pick alerts) where you've enabled them.
  • To improve and maintain the App.

How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure third-party database and authentication provider, on your behalf. We apply access controls so that you can only see your own data and the gang data you're entitled to. Passwords are encrypted and are never visible to us.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your picks and tail/fade activity are visible to members of gangs you join, that's the core purpose of the App. We use service providers (such as Supabase for data storage) only to operate the App. We may disclose information if required by law.

Affiliate Links

The App may contain links to third-party sportsbooks. If you follow a link and sign up or deposit, we may earn a commission. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. We encourage you to review them.

Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete Account). Deleting your account permanently removes your account and your personal data, including your bets, picks, and gang memberships. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access or correct your data; contact us to exercise them.

Children

SlipGang is intended for adults of legal betting age and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
